About

Hi, I'm Ale.

I have spent twenty years inside international organisations, working through the practical challenges of turning agreed priorities into work that people can actually carry forward. Give me a tangled problem and a room full of people who see it differently, and I am in my element.

I am endlessly curious and a forever-student, always learning something new and asking what we are really trying to solve. I like getting beneath the first explanation, connecting the dots and working with people to find a response that makes sense in their world.

For me, that means making change something people are part of. I want to understand how they experience the work, what they have already tried and what would help them move it forward.

Off the clock, I'm a mum, a cat person 🐱 (don't worry, I love dogs too), and happiest under water. I'm a certified Divemaster 🤿 🐠, and the ocean is where I connect my own dots.
