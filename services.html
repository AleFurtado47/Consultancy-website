Specialist support
within an engagement.

Depending on what the work requires, an engagement can draw on my experience in the areas below. We agree the relevant support and its scope together.

Delivery plans and results frameworks

Connect priorities to the work teams plan and use results information to support delivery decisions. Assessment identifies what needs attention; developing and putting changes into use requires the corresponding engagement scope.

The plans come first: priorities carried down into unit plans and individual objectives, so that what was agreed reaches the appraisal system that tells staff what they are rewarded for. A framework built to report to funders will rarely tell management anything it can act on, and turning it into one that does means giving every indicator the threshold that forces action and the person who acts once it is crossed.

Digital and AI in practice

Examine where digital tools or AI could help the work, alongside the data, governance and working practices needed to use them responsibly.

Governance first, starting with data, so the tools make work easier rather than adding a layer nobody asked for.

Advising leaders and boards

Work through difficult delivery decisions, clarify responsibilities and examine governance arrangements with the people who hold approval authority.

A trusted outside head for leaders facing reform or ambiguity: naming the real problem and working out the answer together.

How I work

How engagements run.

01

Diagnose with the team

The people doing the work usually know what is blocking it: I ask, then resolve the systemic constraint behind the answer.

02

Adoption over rollout

A plan or tool that is not used does not count, so uptake is measured and followed through after every rollout.

03

Evidence over impressions

Indicators and dashboards are built into everything I run, so results reach boards and funders as data rather than anecdotes.

04

Develop people through the work

Training runs alongside delivery rather than apart from it, with coaching that moves people into bigger roles.

05

Multicultural by default

Twenty years leading teams across headquarters, regional and country operations in 20+ countries, in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
