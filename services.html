Where to start

How I usually start.

I stage the work rather than selling one package at three sizes, because organisations arrive here from different starting points and the right first step depends on what you already have in place. Each step below stands on its own, so nothing commits you to the one after it.

Step 01

Delivery Readiness Review

3 weeks · fixed fee

A strategy has been approved and the open question is whether the organisation can deliver it. Working back from the outcomes the strategy promised to the people it serves, I trace five mechanisms and report which of them support delivery, which constrain it, and in what order the constraints have to be lifted.

Instrument · The Monday-Morning Test

You get a short report naming what will stall delivery, and the first move on each. See the method and two case studies · Download the product sheet

Step 02

Operating Model Alignment

8 to 12 weeks

Where the review finds that the strategy cannot command the work, this rebuilds the connection between them: decision rights that say who decides what, prioritisation criteria that name what stops rather than only what starts, and a portfolio view of the whole.

Step 03

Delivery Plans & Results Frameworks

The plans come first: priorities carried down into unit plans and individual objectives, so that what was approved reaches the appraisal system that tells staff what they are rewarded for.

A framework built to report to funders will rarely tell management anything it can act on. This turns it into one that does, by giving every indicator the threshold that forces action and the person who acts once it is crossed.
