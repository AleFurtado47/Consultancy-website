The gap

Where strategy stops.

The body that approves isn’t the body that delivers. A Board or Council signs off; country offices and technical divisions carry it.
The budget contradicts the strategy. Earmarked funding locks capacity to yesterday’s priorities regardless of what was approved.
Nobody owns the outcome. Matrix structures split accountability across regions, functions and programmes until it belongs to no one.
The results framework reports, it doesn’t decide. Built to satisfy donors, so nobody uses it to steer.
Governance meets to report status, not to decide.

The strategy isn’t the problem. The system it lands in is.

About

Hi, I'm Ale.

Strategy doesn't fail on paper. It fails on Monday morning, when teams go back to work and nothing has actually changed for them. I've spent twenty years inside international organizations closing that gap, and I've come to love both halves of the job: building a strategy that can survive a matrix structure and an earmarked budget, and doing the unglamorous work of making one that's already been approved actually move. Give me a tangled problem and a room full of people who disagree about it, and I'm in my element.

I'm endlessly curious and a forever-student, always learning something new, always asking what we're really trying to solve. I simplify, I connect the dots, and I make change feel like something people are part of, not something happening to them.

Off the clock, I'm a mum, a cat person 🐱 (don't worry, I love dogs too), and happiest under water. I'm a certified Divemaster 🤿🐠, and the ocean is where I connect my own dots.
