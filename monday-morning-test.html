The instrument

The Monday-Morning Test

Choose a strategy or plan and answer five questions about the arrangements supporting its delivery. Your reading explores what your answers suggest and where a closer look may help.

Your answers and optional name stay in this tab. They are not stored or sent.

What would you like to examine?

This is an exploratory use. The questions may not fit every kind of work.

The five mechanisms

  1. AuthorshipWho developed the substance?
  2. BudgetDoes funded work cover required work?
  3. OwnershipWhere are formal commitments held?
  4. ReportingWhat does delivery mean for outcomes?
  5. DecidingWhere is authority exercised?

Hold the same strategy or plan in mind throughout, and answer for how things work in practice rather than how they were designed.

Five answers from one person provide a first reading, not a diagnosis. They do not establish causes, what needs attention first or whether delivery will succeed.

      About the Review

      The Delivery Readiness Review

      Your reading reflects five answers from one person. The Review examines relevant records alongside the experience of the people responsible for the work, to understand what is supporting delivery and what may be holding it back.

      What your organisation receives

      An evidence-based assessment of the delivery constraints, with recommended next steps and a clear account of what remains uncertain. The findings provide a basis for deciding what needs attention first.

      The Review is a complete assessment engagement. Your organisation can act on the findings internally; response design and implementation support can be commissioned separately where needed.

      Prior institutional experience

      100+ projects · 20+ countries

      I designed and led changes across this portfolio, working with teams to revise planned work and improve how delivery decisions were made.
